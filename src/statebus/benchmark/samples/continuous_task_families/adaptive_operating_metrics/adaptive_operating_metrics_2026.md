# Meridian Distribution 2026 Operating Metrics Review

Document id: `doc-meridian-adaptive-metrics-2026`

Meridian Distribution supplies regional fulfillment and returns processing for
consumer electronics retailers. The review covers segment revenue and delivery
reliability across the 2026 operating plan. The tables are the authoritative
numeric evidence for bounded analysis tasks; the surrounding narrative is only
business context for retrieval and cited reporting.

## Segment Revenue Table

| quarter | segment | revenue_musd |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 2026Q1 | enterprise | 82 |
| 2026Q2 | enterprise | 88 |
| 2026Q3 | enterprise | 91 |
| 2026Q1 | consumer | 43 |
| 2026Q2 | consumer | 46 |
| 2026Q3 | consumer | 49 |

Enterprise demand grew as two national retailers expanded managed returns
coverage. Consumer revenue remained smaller but increased as regional pickup
locations adopted the same tracking service.

## Delivery Reliability Table

| quarter | on_time_delivery_pct |
| --- | ---: |
| 2025Q4 | 96.1 |
| 2026Q1 | 95.4 |
| 2026Q2 | 94.8 |
| 2026Q3 | 71.2 |

The final quarter included a carrier handoff incident affecting weekend lanes.
Operations needs a bounded anomaly review to decide whether the reliability
change requires a cited risk memo.

## Metric Definitions

`revenue_musd` is recognized segment revenue in millions of US dollars.
`on_time_delivery_pct` is the share of orders delivered within the committed
customer window during the quarter.
