## Overview

This report covers quarterly revenue figures for ACME Corporation and BETA Inc across three quarters each (2025Q3, 2025Q4, 2026Q1). It is designed for cross-period trend analysis and cross-company comparison benchmarks.

ACME sustained consistent revenue growth across the three reported quarters. Revenue increased from 98 million in 2025Q3 to 109 million in 2025Q4 and reached 120 million in 2026Q1, representing approximately 11 percent quarter-over-quarter growth in each period.

ACME revenue exceeded BETA revenue in all three quarters. The gap remained stable: ACME led by 26 million in 2025Q3, 30 million in 2025Q4, and 33 million in 2026Q1. Both companies show parallel upward trends.
