# Delta Hub Conditional Rail Release Memo

Document id: `doc-delta-rail-release-memo`

## Scope

This memo records the dispatch authority for Delta Hub after the throughput
review. It does not revise the historical throughput observations and does not
create a forecast beyond 2026Q2. Its only purpose is to define when the
capacity-capped outbound volume may be released.

## Rail Condition

The rail operator offered a conditional allocation for the Wednesday outbound
movement. Delta must submit the final wagon manifest by Tuesday at 16:00 local
time. The allocation becomes firm only after the operator acknowledges that
manifest. A draft manifest, an email reservation, or available truck capacity
does not satisfy the release condition.

If acknowledgment arrives by the deadline, the additional outbound volume may
be released on Wednesday morning. The dispatch lead must retain the operator's
acknowledgment with the shipment record so that the release can be audited.

## Relationship To Truck Capacity

Truck availability remains sufficient for ordinary committed shipments. That
fact does not override the pending rail-slot approval for the additional
capacity-capped volume. The extra volume was consolidated for the rail movement,
and releasing it without the acknowledged allocation would break the approved
yard and loading sequence.

The logistics team may continue normal truck dispatches while the condition is
pending. It may not label the extra rail volume as an ordinary truck shipment
to bypass the approval gate.

## Missed-Deadline Fallback

If the Tuesday 16:00 acknowledgment is missed, Delta must keep the additional
volume capacity-capped and move it to the next Thursday rail window. Urgent
customer orders already inside the ordinary truck plan may continue, but no
new forecast or unapproved release may be inferred from this exception.

## Evidence Boundary

The Delta Hub Throughput Review establishes that rail-slot approval is the
binding qualifier and that truck availability is not the limiting resource.
This memo supplies the exact deadline, Wednesday release timing, and Thursday
fallback. Both records are required for the complete dispatch conclusion.
