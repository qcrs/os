# Meridian North Coast Response Authorization

Document id: `doc-meridian-response-authorization`

## Purpose

This note records the decision made after the spring distribution review. It is
an authorization record, not a second operating diagnosis. The signed review
remains authoritative for the source of demand, the physical bottleneck, and
the delivery risk. This note states which response may be used and the limits
attached to that response.

## Decision Context

The North Coast team asked for additional coverage during the evening sort.
Finance did not approve a permanent head-count increase because the hospital
restocking pattern may normalize after the next replenishment cycle. Facilities
also rejected a permanent cold-storage expansion: the lead time would extend
beyond the planning window and would not address the immediate weekend gap.

The authorization assumes that dry-trailer supply and line-haul capacity remain
adequate. It does not authorize the team to reinterpret a refrigerated-door
constraint as a general transport shortage. If that assumption changes, the
regional director must open a separate review rather than silently broadening
this decision.

## Authorized Response

Management authorized a temporary rotating weekend dock team for two
replenishment waves. The first rotation must be in place before the next
replenishment wave. Supervisors may rearrange weekday shifts to staff the
rotation, but they may not create permanent positions under this approval.

The response is deliberately temporary. After two replenishment waves, the
regional director must compare refrigerated-door queue time, night-shift
utilization, and delayed-delivery exceptions with the signed spring review.
Continuation requires a new authorization.

## Activation And Guardrails

The weekend rotation is activated only while weekend dock coverage remains
unchanged and the signed review's delayed-delivery risk remains open. If the
existing carrier and dock schedule is revised before the next wave, operations
must confirm that the original risk still exists before using the overtime
budget.

The authorization caps overtime at 180 hours across the two waves. It permits
temporary shift rotation and queue coordination, but it does not permit a
permanent facility expansion, a standing third shift, or a change to product
temperature controls.

## Audit Boundary

This note and the signed Meridian Distribution Network Review must be read
together. The review supplies the causal evidence and proposed mitigation; this
note supplies approval scope, timing, and duration. Neither document alone
authorizes a broader network redesign.
