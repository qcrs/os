# ACME Operations And Financial Review 2026

Document id: `doc-acme-ops-2026`

## Executive Overview

ACME expanded revenue during the first three quarters of 2026 while facing supply-chain pressure in the appliance division and churn pressure in the enterprise software unit. The finance team reports that revenue increased from 120 million in Q1 to 132 million in Q2 and 145 million in Q3. Gross margin remained above 40 percent in each quarter but declined in Q3 because expedited freight costs increased.

## Churn Narrative

Enterprise software churn increased in Q3. The customer success team attributed the increase to delayed onboarding for two large accounts and to a migration backlog after the Q2 release. Account notes show that customers with delayed onboarding had a churn rate that was 1.1 percentage points higher than the broader enterprise base.

## Supply Chain Narrative

The appliance division used expedited freight in August and September after a supplier tooling issue delayed shipments. The operations team reports that on-time delivery fell from 94.0 percent in Q1 to 88.0 percent in Q3. The mitigation plan moved two high-volume parts to a secondary supplier and added a weekly supplier-risk review.

## Management Actions

Management approved three actions for Q4: reduce onboarding backlog by assigning a dedicated migration squad, rebalance inventory toward the secondary supplier, and review freight contracts before peak season. The expected benefit is lower churn pressure and improved on-time delivery.
