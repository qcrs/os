# Meridian Distribution Network Review

This offline review combines commercial and operating observations from the spring planning cycle. It is intentionally narrative: no answer table or key-value appendix is included.

## Market signal

The operating region was North Coast corridor. Hospital customers there brought forward replenishment requests after two distributors shortened their normal ordering windows. The change was concentrated in temperature-controlled product lines rather than the broader catalog.

## Commercial backdrop

Contract renewals remained within the annual plan. Account teams reported that price was not the limiting factor, and no material customer cancellation was recorded during the review window.

## Fulfillment constraint

The binding constraint was limited cold-storage dock availability. Trailer supply and line-haul capacity remained adequate, but inbound and outbound loads competed for the same refrigerated doors during the evening sort.

## Inventory observation

Finished-goods coverage stayed above the internal minimum. A temporary packaging shortage affected a separate inland site and did not alter the North Coast fulfillment decision.

## Causal chain

The primary demand cause was an accelerated hospital restocking cycle. The operational effect was night-shift utilization above the planned ceiling. Both observations were recorded before any mitigation was approved.

## Risk response

The conditional risk was delayed regional deliveries if weekend dock coverage remains unchanged. The stated mitigation was add a rotating weekend dock team before the next replenishment wave. Management did not authorize a permanent facility expansion.

## Audit note

The review was assembled from signed operating notes and retained for offline reproducibility. All statements above belong to the authorized source document.
